Support

SportsGuy gives you one short, plain-English sports take about your local teams, so you can hold your own when the game comes up at the bar. No stats, no jargon, just something you can say.

Need help? Email tommyshimko@gmail.com and a real person (Tommy, who made it) will write back.

Questions

How are takes made?

When you ask for one, Anthropic's Claude AI searches today's sports news for your city and sport, writes a short take from what it finds, and then a second check reads the take against those news sources before you see it.

Why is a take wrong sometimes?

It's written by AI from the news of the day. News can be late, a game might still be going, and AI can still slip up even after the check. If one looks off, email us what it said. It helps a lot.

How many takes do I get?

Signed in, you get 5 free new takes every day. Takes other people in your city already pulled up are free to replay any time and don't count.

How do I change my sport or city?

You can switch sports any time from the main screen. To change your city, open the location picker and either use your current location or type a city or ZIP code.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings > Delete account. It's instant. You can also ask us by email.

Is my data safe?

We keep very little and never sell it. There are no ads or trackers. The details are in our privacy policy.
