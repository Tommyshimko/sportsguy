Delete your account

You can delete your SportsGuy account whenever you like. It removes your account, your email, the teams you follow and your take balance. This can't be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SportsGuy.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It's gone right away.

Without the app

Email tommyshimko@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject:

Delete my SportsGuy account

We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to let you know.

If you used Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", send it from the Apple relay address, or tell us that's what you used and we'll sort it out with you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • The teams you follow
  • Your take balance

Takes are shared by city and aren't linked to you, so there's nothing personal left behind. Our host's short-lived server logs expire on their own. More in our privacy policy.
